Five Cones

Two coherent models, A and B, are cones of accepted gambles. Five thin wedges a to e are the patches. X ⪯₁ Y means X is at least as accurate as Y at world 1.

The four comparisons world 1 must license

Licence 1

World 1 must rank A ⪯₁ A ∪ a ∪ (−c)

That is, A is at least as accurate at world 1 as the rival A ∪ a ∪ (−c). The reverse comparison is not ranked, so A comes out strictly better.

  • a: A is silent; the rival accepts every g in a. rival better at world 1.
  • c: A is silent; the rival accepts −g for every g in c. rival worse at world 1.

The rival agrees with A everywhere else. World 2 sees each change the other way round, so it ranks neither model above the other.

Licence 2

World 1 must rank B ⪯₁ B ∖ (b ∪ −c)

That is, B is at least as accurate at world 1 as the rival B ∖ (b ∪ −c). The reverse comparison is not ranked, so B comes out strictly better.

  • b: B accepts every g in b; the rival is silent. rival worse at world 1.
  • c: B accepts −g for every g in c; the rival is silent. rival better at world 1.

The rival agrees with B everywhere else. World 2 sees each change the other way round, so it ranks neither model above the other.

Licence 3

World 1 must rank A ⪯₁ A ∪ b ∪ (−d)

  • b: A is silent; the rival accepts every g in b. rival better at world 1.
  • d: A is silent; the rival accepts −g for every g in d. rival worse at world 1.

Licence 4

World 1 must rank B ⪯₁ B ∖ (a ∪ −d ∪ −e)

That is, B is at least as accurate at world 1 as the rival B ∖ (a ∪ −d ∪ −e). The reverse comparison is not ranked, so B comes out strictly better.

  • a: B accepts every g in a; the rival is silent. rival worse at world 1.
  • d, e: B accepts −g for every g in d and e; the rival is silent. rival better at world 1.

The rival agrees with B everywhere else. World 2 sees each change the other way round, so it ranks neither model above the other.

No positive additive penalties can support all four. Write w_x for the penalty attached to one step of advice change on patch x. The four licences would need w_c ≥ w_a, w_b ≥ w_c, w_d ≥ w_b and w_a ≥ w_d + w_e, which together give w_a ≥ w_a + w_e.
