Points for the lead:
- The LDG manuscript does not formally define guidance, independence or local freedom; they belong to the covering paper. The map therefore describes them generically, as constraints that applications add to the class of reasonable rankings and to the domains, and says the abstract results do not use them.
- The rescaling sentence is stated for pointwise minimisers of fixed weighted combinations. It does not claim invariance of the constrained minimisers in Prop. lexbayes, because the zero set of a first-level score need not be preserved unless that integrand is pointwise nonnegative.
