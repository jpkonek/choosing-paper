## Binding answers (audit)
- **(IB) vs (S).** Verdicts ALWAYS depend only on the record ((IB)); this is never dropped. (S) requires closed acceptance sections for a FIXED rival as the selected source varies. Neither requires closure under arbitrary convergent sequences of records.
- **Closure computations.** Use the LEAST closure under the actual rules: common-context copying, the required section limits and (TC), ITERATED together. One pass is insufficient. A closure check is a verification; the proof must identify a PROPERTY that survives every application of those rules.
- **Second defenders.**
  - They may be ANY coherent models. Rescalings and vertex-wise rescalings are candidate generators, not restrictions.
  - Every committed defender gets an explicit place in the partial selection, with sections checked in the resulting index topology.
  - No isolated index merely to make sections vacuous.
  - No assumption that the index equals the likelihood or lies in a 2-D posterior neighbourhood.
- **Invariant.** The endorsed base is only an initial source of possible defences. Preserve guidance and whatever the joint proof needs. OTHER base acceptances may be WITHDRAWN, provided their defence obligations are reassigned consistently. Record:
  - accepted comparisons;
  - refused comparisons, with their fixed-rival neighbourhoods;
  - the remaining defending worlds for each source–rival obligation;
  - the committed coherent models and selection indices.
  
  An extension must preserve their JOINT consistency, not just show each new refusal is individually harmless. PP74's "no consecutive dominations" is a sufficient device where available, not a requirement. If two dominations compose, examine the triangle.
- **Mixed refusals.**
  - For each fixed rival X, {p : C_p ⋠_i X} must be open. This is a condition on the complete allocation.
  - At every selected source, the surviving defences must coexist across both orientations of records, (TC) consequences, common-context copies, section limits, protection and the actual all-world tie condition.
  - Records carried in BOTH directions are NOT prohibited.
- **Choose a defence and its alternatives together.** When a domination forces a mixed refusal at a source, output an alternative defending world, and prove that installing it does not destroy another required defence.
