**There is useful progress in diagnosing the chosen construction, but not yet significant progress towards the milestone.** The round has not narrowed the general problem to one step. It has narrowed a heavily restricted construction to several interacting problems.

I read PP37, PP38, PP40 and PP41. I would change the emphasis of round 50 rather than proceed with the proposed plan unchanged.

### What has genuinely improved

Two findings are worth keeping.

- **PP40 identifies the missing compatibility condition in PP38.** An accepted repair can conflict with an ordinary field-positive comparison through a strict guidance step. Compensation and the absence of mutually inverse repair records do not suffice. This explains why the earlier extension claim failed.
- **PP41’s common-valuation obstruction has a substantive mechanism.** Under its stated additional hypotheses, closed sections force equalities that are incompatible with the relevant optimisation conditions. This is more informative than a failed numerical search.

But neither supplies the missing recursive construction. Universal competitiveness, undominatedness, cross-gate (TC), closed sections and the required dominations still have not been established together.

### The main strategy error: treating the chosen kernel as settled

PP38’s kernel accepts a bounded record exactly when one fixed additive field accepts it. Its own Theorem 3.1 then forces every registered model to be Bayes for that field.

For the particular field chosen, the permitted coherent models are only:

\[
T,\qquad B_{ij}(t),\qquad V_k.
\]

**That is why the path must jump.** The construction has excluded the intermediate coherent models that might otherwise connect T to the boundary.

The status report consequently overstates two conclusions:

- “The jump is the crux” should read **“the jump is the crux after fixing this bounded kernel.”**
- “Every single-valuation design is ruled out” needs PP41’s precise scope: the kernel, the post-jump family matching the milestone edge, the installed-record restriction, and the valuation assumptions.

PP41 expressly leaves the different-post-jump-family case open. More fundamentally, changing the bounded kernel changes the available coherent family altogether.

The kernel has not been shown extendable merely because it passes the bounded tests. **Its effect on the available defenders is part of its cost, not a separate geometric inconvenience.**

### Two proof issues to repair now

These are additional to the proposed referee tasks.

**1. PP41’s hypothesis (NA) is impossible as written.**

It requires the values of bounded perturbations to fill an entire open half-space. But the field has an integrable envelope and only four advice options. Consequently, at each world,

\[
|\Phi_w(r)|
\leq
\int \left(\max_a\ell_w(g,a)-\min_a\ell_w(g,a)\right)\,d\mu(g),
\]

uniformly over all records. The attainable vectors are bounded; an open half-space is not.

A local, small-scale version might support a repaired argument. That would require proving simultaneous attainability with suitable scales and disjoint supports. Until then, Proposition 3.4′ does not establish necessity of common Bayes weights in the proposed setting, and Proposition 2.8’s dependence on it remains unresolved.

**2. PP41 Proposition 4.1(b) does not establish its stated protection against all-world ties.**

Its proof says an all-world tie through C’s own valuation forces that valuation to vanish. But the relation is a union of gates. Other gates can accept the reverse comparisons even when C’s own gate strictly favours C.

The valid conclusion concerns ties endorsed in both directions by the same valuation. It does not establish direct (C) for the union. The note later acknowledges that other gates may create domination; the earlier claim needs to be reconciled with that acknowledgment.

Also, do not use the assertion that certain splice sets form a “null family” without defining a measure on that family and proving the required range-preservation result. PP41 currently labels the weakening of its installed-record hypothesis open; retain the explicit hypothesis.

### What I recommend for round 50

**Make revising the bounded kernel the main construction task. Keep the fixed-kernel gate approach as a secondary, sharply bounded test.**

The objective should be:

> Construct bounded and unbounded comparisons jointly, so that adding the coherent defenders needed by the repair operation does not first require proving that they are Bayes for one preselected field.

This does not mean abandoning every additive comparison. It means abandoning the commitment to **all** of one field’s bounded verdicts.

Give the main task these specific instructions:

1. **Make both the defender and the affected bounded verdicts variables.**  
   When a candidate coherent defender is excluded by PP38’s Bayes restriction, identify the actual bounded comparisons responsible. Decide whether they can be refused or reassigned while preserving the existing defence obligations. Do not discard the defender solely because it is not \(\ell^T\)-Bayes.

2. **Use the ordinal refusal theorem directly.**  
   For an accepted domination \(C\preceq_v Y\), enforce the refusals against strict guidance improvements of C. Do not replace these requirements by the stronger numerical budget unless that budget is deliberately chosen and its consequences accepted.

3. **Keep the existing milestone.**  
   Registered models still need universal competitiveness; the edge and connecting path still need closed fixed-rival sections. Do not weaken those requirements to make a new bounded rule appear successful.

4. **Test the first recursive repair that needs an excluded model.**  
   Use a backward rival, a T-fill, or the midpoint edge model’s forced rival. Allow refinements, coarsenings, segments and other coherent defenders. The question is whether changing the responsible bounded verdicts permits the repair **without destroying previous defences after closure**.

5. **Do not impose a continuous model-valued path.**  
   A richer family may remove the jump, but that is an opportunity, not a new axiom. Closed sections remain the requirement.

For the **secondary fixed-kernel task**, demand one concrete result before further expansion:

> Construct two distinct registered models with their proposed separate valuations, include every common-context copy of their accepted comparisons, and prove cross-gate (TC), undominatedness and the relevant section inheritance simultaneously.

Do not count “each model is competitive through its own gate” as success. That part is already available; the interaction is the unresolved problem.

Finally, any refusal must be a **global verdict on the disagreement record**. Refusing it through one realiser’s gate while allowing another gate to accept the identical record is not a refusal. State the aggregation rule explicitly.

**My recommendation is therefore to remove the frozen bounded kernel before investing the main effort in increasingly elaborate ways to jump out of its restricted coherent family.** Model-specific valuations are worth testing, but retaining the common kernel preserves precisely the restriction that generated the jump and the shortage of dominators. The more ambitious co-design step is to let a needed defender change the ranking’s bounded comparisons as well as its unbounded ones—and prove that the existing models retain their defences.
