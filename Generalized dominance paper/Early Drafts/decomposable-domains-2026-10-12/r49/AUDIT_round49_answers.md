There is one important refinement to the proposed invariant. **Nonempty pair-dependent world sets alone are insufficient.** They certify possible defences against the proposed dominators, but not against arbitrary rivals, and several individually possible choices may conflict when made together. The invariant must include an actual simultaneous allocation—or a proved extension property for those allocations—not just a list of individually available worlds.
