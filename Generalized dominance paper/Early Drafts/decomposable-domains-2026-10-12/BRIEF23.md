## Main objective (audit)
Construct three independent ordinal rankings TOGETHER with their coherent selection on the WHOLE simplex, containing a VERIFIED failure of additive endorsement on comparisons out of coherent undominated models. T1 should ideally be selected.

1. **Build all three rankings jointly.** Leave competitiveness as a choice among worlds. Do NOT keep two additive partners, and do NOT keep every strict comparison of a fixed additive score.
2. **Start from a NON-PRICEABLE SEED.** This is a finite collection of comparisons, out of coherent (to-be-undominated) models, whose weighted sum violates strict guidance (a positive combination ≤ 0 and ≠ 0), although no genuine composition of them produces a guidance violation.
   - Such a seed rules out every strictly positive finitely additive endorser.
   - Weighted summation is a diagnostic only. The orders close under genuine composition alone.
   - Known constraints on the seed:
     - **K2.1 (divisibility):** the seed must contain rigid, non-divisible comparisons.
     - **N17:** at least 4 comparisons, with overlapping gains and crossing error sets.
     - **K2.2:** comparisons forced next to leading-density worlds are divisible. So do not put leading-density partners next to the seed.
3. **The central theorem: an EXPLICIT INVARIANT.** The seed must satisfy it, and each extension step must preserve it. The extension steps are: adding comparisons, adding competitors (the competitiveness disjunctions), composition closure, closed sections (through a fixed reservation plan), protection of selected models, and the no-(C)-ties rule.
   - "Every finite fragment is satisfiable" is NOT acceptable as the invariant; it restates the goal.
   - The invariant must be checkable independently.

A finite certificate is a construction target. It is not a necessary condition for coverage.
