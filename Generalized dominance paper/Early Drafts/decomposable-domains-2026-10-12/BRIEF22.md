## Task A: a full three-world construction (positive)
A full three-world system on ALL measurable models and the WHOLE simplex, any coherent target (an easy one is acceptable), every axiom, in which some world does NOT add up. Safeguards:
1. **Relevant comparisons.** The non-endorsement must occur on RELEVANT comparisons: those out of coherent undominated (selected) models.
2. **Not an endpoint device.** The certificate must not be another endpoint blow-up. Preferred:
   - a FINITE CANCELLATION certificate (positive combination of relevant comparison vectors ≤ 0 and ≠ 0); or
   - an infinitary certificate confined to a FIXED region of mixed problems away from the coordinate planes.
3. **Aimed at T1.** Identify from the outset how the competitive coherent family might admit T1.

If the construction fails, the failure must be an explicit incompatibility forced by the axioms or by shared closed-section obligations, not the failure of a particular scoring recipe.
