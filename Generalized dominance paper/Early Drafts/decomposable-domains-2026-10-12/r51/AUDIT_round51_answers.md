**Your defaults are mostly right. Allow T’s field to change, vary the supporting weights, and test an explicit index map now.** Two qualifications matter: do not build an unproved directional interpretation into that map, and distinguish auxiliary gated models from selected models.

1. **Index map: choose (b), without prescribing its direction.**

   Construct the fields on the parameter box, and propose an explicit selection from a two-dimensional neighbourhood of T’s index into that box. Check closed sections there for **every measurable rival**.

   However, “tilting the index towards \(i\) should cut the other corners” is a design intuition, not an axiom. Interior indices all have the same support. Our axioms do not identify the index with a supporting Bayes weight or require the model to improve at \(i\) as \(p_i\) increases.

   Try that map if useful, but do not reject a construction because its geometry violates this intuition.

   Also, if compatibility makes every gated cube member undominated, those members are part of the actual undominated class—even if the proposed local selection misses them. Keep that fact visible. A partial selection need not exhaust them, but a later full system must address them.

2. **Yes, T’s field may change.**

   Preserve **T’s model and the axioms**, not \(\ell^T\)’s existing verdicts. Treat T’s field as the \(s=0\) member of the jointly designed family.

   Requiring \(\ell^T\) at the origin would retain another potentially unnecessary commitment. You may use it as an initial candidate or comparison, but not as a fixed boundary condition.

   The useful continuity requirement is continuity of the fields’ **advice-cost differences**, with an integrable bound sufficient for the section proof. Additive constants in the penalties have no effect on rankings.

3. **Additive gates first; no automatic lexicographic fallback.**

   The interval criterion is a concrete advantage of this construction. Exploit it before changing the architecture.

   A failed pair or triple should first prompt changes to:

   - the fields on the other corner bands;
   - the supporting weights;
   - the candidate defender or selected subfamily;
   - the strength of the sufficient compatibility conditions.

   In particular, failure of strict CG3 does not prove failure of (TC). Check whether the actual offending records form a forbidden configuration.

   A lexicographic alternative is permitted as a separately labelled branch, but it needs its own proof of cross-gate compatibility and closed sections. Calling it a fallback must not import those conclusions from the additive argument.

4. **Yes, supporting weights may vary freely.**

   Write them separately as \(\lambda_{\mathbf s}\), distinct from the selection index \(p\). For an interior index, any supporting probability weight is allowed, including a boundary weight if it establishes the required unique optimum. More generally, its support must lie within the index’s support for the standard Bayes defence argument.

   There is no required direction of variation. Increasing weight on a world whose corner is cut may actually work against optimality when that cut introduces wrong answers there. Let the inequalities determine the weights.

   Positive rescaling of each world’s entire field preserves that gate’s verdicts and can be absorbed into its supporting weights. Use this freedom to normalise the equations rather than counting equivalent fields as different constructions.

5. **Your proposed repair class is appropriate, with one addition.**

   Include the listed fills, retreats and improved retreats for all members in scope, including arbitrary bounded measurable sets and arbitrarily small changes. Require explicit domination only when the modification is nontrivial and incoherent.

   **Also include one simultaneous two-corner modification**, such as
   \[
   X=T[G_i\mapsto C_{\mathbf s},\;G_j\mapsto C_{\mathbf t}],
   \]
   where the disjoint sets lie in different corner bands. The answer on one band must not be forced to come from the same original member as the answer on the other.

   This directly tests the reason for introducing combined cuts: can the construction coordinate repairs that previously belonged to separate paths?

   Vertex rivals and extension to the edge may be deferred in this round. Universal competitiveness of every registered member against **all** measurable rivals may not be deferred. That remains distinct from the restricted class receiving explicit dominators.

For the work allocation, make the four-model configuration the common first checkpoint: **T, two separate caps, and their combined cut**, with the simultaneous two-corner rival included. The constructor, adversary and computation should examine the same configuration.

The intended deliverable is a formula or extension mechanism valid for a nontrivial range of cut sizes, with compatible fields, universal defence obligations and closed sections—not merely feasible fields at eight sampled models. If that fails, identify whether the obstruction comes from the axioms, the gate-union architecture, or the stronger sufficient compatibility test. Those are three materially different outcomes.
