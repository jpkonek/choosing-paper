# Non-additive rankings for T1: exploration map (copy of project doc, 16 October 2026)

Key items for round 54 (full text is in the CCT project doc claude/non-additive-rankings-exploration-map-2026-10-16.md):

## 2a. Exclusions
1. **Rankings that one bounded weighting can summarise ("adds up", case M0).** At every world, some bounded weight, positive on every non-null set of mixed problems, endorses every relevant comparison.
   - The class includes:
     - credal unanimity (CP-a);
     - lexicographic orders with additive tie-breakers;
     - additive orders with ties broken ordinally;
     - glued credal worlds;
     - intersections of additive rankings.
   - Excluded as FULL covers: on M_U by Theorem M0closed and L4.2; on every (LF) decomposable domain by Q4.1–Q4.2. Intersections have a separate short proof on every (LF) domain.
2. **Complete worlds.** With three complete worlds, the vacuous model is always dominated, so full coverage NEEDS incomparability.
3. **Veto and lexicographic designs.** The veto–Brier ranking VB satisfies every axiom except closed sections, which fail at boundary weightings. "Vetoes cannot be paid" (L3.4).
4. **Decorations of an additive base:** excluded in several forms (H1.2, E1.4, H2.3, J1, K1, U1).
5. **Structure any full cover must have.**
   - Hahn–Banach fixed point (L4, Q3.25): the endorsers of any ranking form an undefeated field, so a cover forces that field to be degenerate. Some world gives zero ordinary weight to a non-null region (a "blind set").
   - Excluded within that requirement: one blind world at an interior weighting (L1); the T1-shadow pattern; any blind target (N1, N2).
   - The reduction R21 lists the survivors. All of them need two or more worlds without strictly positive integrable endorsers, or the vertex configuration (V).
6. **Splicing at a pair-indexed weighting.** It is not transitive, and the intersection repairs fail (S).

## 3. Not yet explored
- No non-additive three-world construction exists for any target.
- Every attempt broke in the same place: defining a world's verdicts on comparisons out of interior selected models, whose error sets cross instead of nesting.
- What is missing is a general method for that step.

**Caution for round 54.** These are FULL-cover statements, mostly on M_U or on (LF) decomposable domains. Whether each one constrains a LOCAL module on N_u is a ledger question.
